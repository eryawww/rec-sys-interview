Watch-Threshold Tree Anatomy

The watch_threshold algorithm ranks items by the predicted probability that a user watches past 600 seconds. The prediction is not one tree: it is a sum over 100 boosted trees, each 31 leaves wide and up to 20 levels deep. These three views go from the whole ensemble, to the top of the first tree, to its complete structure.

1 · Overview

Prediction pipeline

Every stage runs in sequence for a single user-item pair. The 100 trees each contribute one leaf value; those are summed, offset by the prior log-odds of the training labels, and squashed into the 0–1 score that orders the catalogue. Values shown are the real trace for user u1 and item i151 (Hotel Transylvania Part 3).

2 · Detail of stage 1.2

Decision path, tree 1 of 100

Five successive tests route this pair to leaf 56. Each box is one node of tree 1; n is how many of the 5,000 training rows reach it. The first split isolates 70 rows on a single title word, and two of the five tests are on age — the only continuous feature, and the one the ensemble splits on most.

2.1 · Complete structure

Booster tree 1, all 61 nodes

The same tree in full: 30 split nodes, 31 leaves, maximum depth 20. Labels are shown for the top four levels and for every node on the traced path. The shape is characteristic — a few wide splits near the root, then a long thin chain, which is what a tree does when it is separating small groups of rows rather than finding a general rule.

Reading the tree

Leaf values are log-odds, not probabilities

  • Each leaf carries an increment between −0.178 and +0.113.
  • The sum of 100 increments for this pair is +1.567.
  • Only after adding the +0.632 baseline and applying the sigmoid does a probability exist.
  • No single tree, and no single leaf, decides the ranking.

Feature reach

age dominates the splits

  • age takes 27.3% of all 3,000 splits.
  • It is the only continuous feature, so a tree can cut it repeatedly at different thresholds.
  • The 213 title unigrams together supply most of the remaining split variety.
  • 84 of the 235 features are never split on at all.

Bound on the output

Structure is fitted to noise

  • The root split separates 70 of 5,000 rows on one title word.
  • Depth 20 with 31 leaves means long chains isolating small groups.
  • Held-out AUC at this threshold is 0.515, against 0.522 for shuffled labels.
  • The tree structure is real; the rule it encodes does not generalise.
